Working approach

AI assists. People remain accountable.

Each project is being strengthened through a professional, evidence-based lifecycle.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Define stakeholders, business rules, risks, boundaries, and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Validate the foundation

    Inspect source data, document provenance, reconcile KPIs, and test assumptions.

  3. 03

    Build controlled assistance

    Use AI for extraction, classification, comparison, drafting, and prioritization—not final approval.

  4. 04

    Test and communicate

    Document limitations, evaluate outputs, capture evidence, and deliver a stakeholder-ready demonstration.

AI may assist with

Drafting, classification, summarization, code scaffolding, test ideas, and debugging explanations.

I remain responsible for

Business rules, source inspection, metric validation, testing, anomaly investigation, limitations, confidentiality, and final approval.

Portfolio projects use synthetic, anonymized, user-owned, or publicly available data. They contain no confidential employer, customer, drawing, pricing, product, or proprietary process information.
